# YZ50 — Demo Day

A Turkish speech model that I trained from scratch, then fine-tuned on my own voice.

The model is one decoder-only GPT (GPT-2 small body: 12 layers, 12 heads, 768 wide, 99.3M parameters).
It reads the characters of a sentence and then predicts the audio, one token at a time.
The audio is in Mimi codec tokens (kyutai/mimi): 12.5 frames per second × 8 codebooks = 100 tokens per second.
There is no cross-attention and no separate text encoder. Text and audio are in one sequence:

```
[BOS] [speaker tag] t e x t ... [SEP] audio tokens ... [EOS]
```

The loss is only on the audio tokens and EOS. The speaker tag is MALE, FEMALE or ME (my voice).

## Presentation

`page/` is the Demo Day presentation (one HTML page, 8 scenes, with audio).
Open it here: **https://0xyene.github.io/yz50/demo-day/page/**

To open it locally, serve the folder and open it in a browser:

```bash
cd page && python3 -m http.server 8792
```

Then go to http://localhost:8792. Keys: → / ← move between scenes, F is full screen, number keys play the clips on a scene.

Scene 7 ("Dene") is live: you type a sentence and the model says it. The model runs on your own computer through `speak/server.py`:

```bash
python3 speak/server.py        # http://127.0.0.1:8793, uses the Apple GPU (MPS) if there is one
```

The server reads 04c checkpoints from `speak/ckpt/` or `~/Downloads/` (`finetune_c_step01500.pt`, about 224 MB, not in this repo). On GitHub Pages this scene is off.

## Notebooks

The notebooks are built from `_build/src/*.py` with `_build/build.py`. They run on Colab (A100) and keep their data on Google Drive.

| # | What it does | File |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Alania synthetic Turkish speech (cc-by config, CC BY 4.0) to Mimi tokens. | `01_prep_alania.ipynb` |
| 2 | Pretraining: 356 hours of speech, 60,000 steps. Final validation loss 3.27 (best 3.24 at step 54,000). | `02_pretrain.ipynb` |
| 3 | My voice from my 12 assignment videos: voice activity detection, Whisper transcripts, Mimi tokens. 1,176 segments, 2.6 hours. | `03_my_voice.ipynb` |
| 4 | Fine-tune on the video audio. | `04_finetune_me.ipynb` |
| 4b | Fine-tune on 300 sentences that I read with my own recording page (`recorder/`). 15.5 minutes of clean audio with exact text. | `04b_finetune_read.ipynb` |
| 4c | The same fine-tune with a lower learning rate and a checkpoint every 100 steps. | `04c_finetune_sweep.ipynb` |
| 5 | Freya-TR-Eval (495 Turkish sentences) with Freya's protocol: faster-whisper large-v3, 8 kHz round trip, WER and CER. | `05_freya_eval.ipynb` |
| 5b | Each 4c checkpoint: WER on 99 Freya sentences and speaker similarity to my real voice (WavLM x-vector, cosine). | `05b_sweep_eval.ipynb` |
| 6 | A test of a voice prompt: a real clip of mine before the sentence. It did not help. | `06_speak_prompt.ipynb` |

`recorder/` has the recording page (`index.html` + `server.py`), the sentence list (`build_sentences.py`, `sentences.json`) and `prep_read.py`, which turns the recordings into Mimi tokens.
No sentence in the list is in Freya-TR-Eval (exact match, containment and shared 5-word sequences were checked).

## Results

Freya-TR-Eval, all 495 sentences (lower is better):

| Model | WER | CER |
|---|---|---|
| My model, pretrained (MALE tag) | 3.45% | 0.98% |
| FreyaTTS-small (183M) * | 8.0% | 3.0% |
| My model, my read voice, step 200 | 8.4% | 2.8% |
| XTTS-v2 (0.5B) * | 11.1% | – |
| My model, my video voice, step 500 | 18.8% | 8.8% |
| F5-TTS (0.3B) * | 24.3% | – |

\* Numbers from the Freya-TR-Eval README.

The pretraining data (Alania) was generated by VoxCPM2 (2B). Part of the clarity comes from that large teacher model.
The benchmark measures only intelligibility, not naturalness. No test sentence is in the training data.

15 minutes of clean read speech gave a lower WER than 2.6 hours of noisy video audio (8.4% against 18.8%).

The 4c sweep (99 sentences per checkpoint) shows the trade-off between clarity and my voice:

| Checkpoint | WER | Similarity to my voice |
|---|---|---|
| Pretrained voice (another male voice) | 3.8% | 0.882 |
| 4c step 300 (clearest) | 5.6% | 0.905 |
| 4c step 1500 (most like me) | 12.2% | 0.925 |
| My real recordings | – | 0.938 |

Raw numbers and clips: `results/eval/` and `results/sweep/`.
